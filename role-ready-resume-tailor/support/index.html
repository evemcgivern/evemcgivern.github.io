How can we help?

RoleReady Resume Tailor helps tailor a resume to a complete job description using facts you provide. It can ask follow-up questions, explain suggested changes, and flag evidence gaps.

Get help

Describe what happened and what you expected. Please do not send a resume, job description, or other sensitive personal information in a support request.

Support is provided by Eve McGivern. A response time is not guaranteed.

Before using a draft

Check every fact and edit yourself before sharing a tailored resume with an employer. RoleReady does not promise an ATS score, an interview, or a job outcome. Document exports and page-count checks depend on the tools available in your ChatGPT setup.
