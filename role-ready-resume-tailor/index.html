The approach

A tailored resume should still sound like your work.

RoleReady Resume Tailor works from your resume and the complete job description. It helps surface the experience that matters for the role, asks questions when details are unclear, and explains the evidence behind suggested changes.

Keep claims grounded

Use only facts supported by the resume and details you confirm. Flag uncertainty instead of filling gaps with invented qualifications.

Show the fit

Bring relevant achievements and role language into clearer focus while keeping the substance of your experience intact.

Explain the edits

Review what changed, why it changed, and where the source material supports the wording.

How to use it

Start with the source material.

  1. Bring both documentsShare your resume and the full job description in your ChatGPT conversation.
  2. Clarify missing detailsAnswer follow-up questions when a claim needs more evidence or context.
  3. Review before usingCheck every fact and edit yourself before sending the resume to an employer.

Know the limits

Useful preparation, not a hiring guarantee.

RoleReady does not promise ATS scores, interviews, or job outcomes. Page count can only be checked when document rendering is available; plain text cannot certify pagination. Export options depend on the tools available in your ChatGPT setup.
